## 1. Background & Context
Currently, **Umiya Graphics and Printing** operates on a traditional, manual print order collection process. Customers physically visit the shop, queue up, submit physical storage drives (USB) or send files via messaging apps (such as WhatsApp/Email), state their print preferences verbally, and wait while the shopkeeper manually calculates costs and processes the print jobs.

---

## 2. Existing Problem & Bottlenecks
1. **Long Customer Waiting Times:** Physical queueing and manual file transfers lead to congestion and wasted time for customers.
2. **Manual Cost & Page Count Errors:** Calculating page counts, custom page ranges, and color vs. B&W pricing manually is error-prone and inefficient during rush hours.
3. **Unorganized Order Tracking:** Lack of real-time status visibility causes customers to repeatedly ask for order updates.
4. **Inefficient Business Record Keeping:** Paper receipts and informal cash logging make daily and monthly revenue tracking difficult.
5. **File Verification Issues:** Corrupted or improperly formatted files are often detected late in the process, wasting paper and printer ink.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution
The **Smart E-Printing Software** is a web-based application designed to digitize, automate, and centralize the entire print ordering and management workflow for Umiya Graphics and Printing.

### Key Objectives:
- **Digitize Print Ordering:** Enable remote document upload and print configuration (Color/B&W, copies, page ranges, paper size).
- **Automate Price Calculation:** Instant, transparent price generation based on server-side document page detection and shopkeeper rates.
- **Streamline Shop Operations:** Provide the shop owner with an interactive dashboard to verify files, accept/reject requests, print jobs, and monitor business analytics.
- **Dual Payment Flexibility:** Support both Cash-on-Pickup and Online Payment Gateways.
- **Real-Time Order Tracking:** Provide live status updates to customers from order submission to final pickup.
