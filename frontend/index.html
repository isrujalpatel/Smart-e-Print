How It Works

From account setup to printed document — 5 simple steps

01

Create Account

Register as a Customer or Shop Owner in seconds. Choose your role and gain access to your personalised dashboard.

02

Upload Document

Upload your PDF or image file. Page count is detected automatically so you get an instant, accurate price estimate.

03

Configure Print

Choose colour or B&W, set number of copies, pick a page range. The total cost updates live as you adjust settings.

04

Owner Reviews

The shop owner sees your order, verifies the file, and accepts or rejects it. You get a real-time status update instantly.

05

Collect & Pay

Visit the shop, collect your freshly printed document, and pay — cash at counter or online through Razorpay. Done.
